## What to look at, in the order it will make sense

### 1. Double-click a module row → the editor opens directly *(the thing you asked for)*
`/forecast-modules` → double-click any row. The read-only panel is **gone**; you land in the edit
form. Also try: **Tab** to a row and press **Enter** — rows are keyboard-reachable now, with a
focus ring. And open the inline **status** dropdown in a row and double-click inside it: it must
*not* throw you into the editor.

*Why the panel went rather than being re-pointed:* six of its rows could never render a value
after CR069 turned flows into stream rows (`Exp Category`, `Yield Spread`, `Yield Spread Entries`,
`Invest`, `Dispose`, and `Growth`, which it read from the wrong key and labelled `%` for a
multiplier). It also showed `Cost Basis 0.00` on the 60 modules that have no balance sheet, and no
streams at all. The read that matters — **View Output** in the editor footer — is unchanged.

### 2. Open an **Expense** module → the form is finally the right size
e.g. `Children`, `Living Expenses`. You should see **General + its stream cards, and nothing

Compare with a **Real Estate** or **Stocks** module — those keep their valuation, because they

### 2b. An Expense module no longer shows a misleading **Account** *(from your QA)*

You spotted that `Car Expenses` showed **Account = Car - Insurance**, which is one of the four
accounts that line covers. It is gone from flow modules, and here is what tracing it found:

- **The engine never read it.** No `account_id` anywhere in `fcbuilder-module.js`. A flow module's
  entries carry the *module's* name (CR069 P0), and the money lands on the **stream's FC line**.
- **`PY Actual` was looking it up in the BALANCE-SHEET report.** All four `Car - *` accounts are
  `profit_loss`, so that field could never show a number for an expense module. It was permanently
  blank, not just wrong.

In its place: **`Actual 2026 — Car Expenses`**, the real spend across *every* account on the line.
For Car Expenses that is **−20,365.47 across 37 transactions**, against the **31,694.02** the
module carries. Check the figure looks right to you — it comes from a new
`GET /fc-lines/actual-totals`, built as the exact sibling of the existing budget query so the two
are comparable.

`Account` is deliberately **not** reported in the residue panel: the column is still live (it
supplies Type/Currency defaults and marks the account taken in the add-from-actuals picker), it is
set on all 60 flow modules, and warning on every one of them would drown the channel.

### 2c. The schedule sections now read as one form with the stream cards *(from your QA)*

Open a **Real Estate** or **Stocks** module and scroll to **Invest / Dispose** (or **Amortization**
on a loan). They were a second visual language on the same form — filled CTA buttons, numbered
circular badges, an SVG empty state, an icon-only delete, ALL-CAPS labels, and inputs a measurable
step chunkier than the stream cards' (0.9rem/0.7rem against 0.875rem/0.35rem). All of that now
matches, with the numbers **copied** from `.fc-stream-card`, not eyeballed.

The last piece is the **coloured left edge**, and it is the only one that carries meaning rather
than polish, so it is read off the engine rather than off the word:

| Section | Edge | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Invest** *(PE: "Capital Call")* | red | `fcbuilder-module.js:534` — `transferValues = -dispose - invest`: cash **out** |
| **Dispose** *(PE: "Distribution")* | green | the same line, disposals stored negative: cash **in** |
| **Amortization** | red | `fcbuilder-loan.js:18` — a schedule row is a repayment: cash **out** |

It is keyed on the **field**, never the label, so a per-type rename cannot flip it — a capital call
is still cash out. **Worth a sanity check from you:** red on *Invest* is the cash direction, not a
judgement about the investment. If that reads wrong, say so — it is a one-line change.

### 3. The safety net: *"Still set — not used by this module"*
**Expect to see nothing.** It is empty on every prod module today, which is how it should ship.

To make it appear on purpose: open a **Stocks** module and add an **Invest** row, save, reopen.
Stocks hides Invest/Dispose (0 of 10 use them on prod) — so the value you just stored shows up in
the residue panel with a **Clear** button, rather than sitting invisible.

**This panel is the whole reason hiding is allowed at all.** CR064 §5 refused per-type forms
because *"a hidden field is not a cleared one"* — and it was right: `PUT` preserves a field the
form stops sending. Nothing is hidden-and-live here; anything hidden that still holds a value is
reported. Clearing takes effect on **Save**, only in the scenario you are editing, and **Cancel
undoes it**.

  *Until v3.14.2 this was impossible:* every module made here became a balance-sheet module
  whatever the type said, and CR041's gate then zeroed its streams.

A seeded card you never fill is **dropped on save**, not stored as a zero-amount row. A card you
add by hand is always kept, even empty.
