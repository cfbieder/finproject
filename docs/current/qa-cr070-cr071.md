## What to look at, in the order it will make sense

### 1. Double-click a module row → the editor opens directly *(the thing you asked for)*
`/forecast-modules` → double-click any row. The read-only panel is **gone**; you land in the edit
form. Also try: **Tab** to a row and press **Enter** — rows are keyboard-reachable now, with a
focus ring. And open the inline **status** dropdown in a row and double-click inside it: it must
*not* throw you into the editor.

*Why the panel went rather than being re-pointed:* six of its rows could never render a value
after CR069 turned flows into stream rows (`Exp Category`, `Yield Spread`, `Yield Spread Entries`,
`Invest`, `Dispose`, and `Growth`, which it read from the wrong key and labelled `%` for a
multiplier). It also showed `Cost Basis 0.00` on the 60 modules that have no balance sheet, and no
streams at all. The read that matters — **View Output** in the editor footer — is unchanged.

### 2. Open an **Expense** module → the form is finally the right size
e.g. `Children`, `Living Expenses`. You should see **General + its stream cards, and nothing
else**. Gone: Cost Basis, Cost Basis (USD), Market Value, Market Value (USD), Growth, Capital
Gains Tax Override, **Sweep Priority**. The engine never read any of them on a flow module.

Compare with a **Real Estate** or **Stocks** module — those keep their valuation, because they
use it.

### 3. The safety net: *"Still set — not used by this module"*
**Expect to see nothing.** It is empty on every prod module today, which is how it should ship.

To make it appear on purpose: open a **Stocks** module and add an **Invest** row, save, reopen.
Stocks hides Invest/Dispose (0 of 10 use them on prod) — so the value you just stored shows up in
the residue panel with a **Clear** button, rather than sitting invisible.

**This panel is the whole reason hiding is allowed at all.** CR064 §5 refused per-type forms
because *"a hidden field is not a cleared one"* — and it was right: `PUT` preserves a field the
form stops sending. Nothing is hidden-and-live here; anything hidden that still holds a value is
reported. Clearing takes effect on **Save**, only in the scenario you are editing, and **Cancel
undoes it**.

### 4. Create a module and pick a type *(this is new behaviour — check it fits how you think)*
**+ New** → set **Type**.

- **Expense / Income** → no valuation section, and one stream card of the right direction.
  *Until v3.14.2 this was impossible:* every module made here became a balance-sheet module
  whatever the type said, and CR041's gate then zeroed its streams.
- **Real Estate** → seeds a `% of value` carry-cost card **and a rent card**. Deliberate: **0 of
  40** Real Estate modules on prod carry any income, while your actuals show `Rental - Spain`
  **+31,306** unmapped. The stream card always supported it; the form never asked.
- **Stocks / Fixed Income** → one yield card.

A seeded card you never fill is **dropped on save**, not stored as a zero-amount row. A card you
add by hand is always kept, even empty.
